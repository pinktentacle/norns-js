   Read that as:

     levels  (16 solid rects, integer-aligned, opaque)  -> 16x pixman_fill, nothing else
     geom    (2 line strokes, 1 arc stroke, 1 curve stroke,
              1 integer rect fill, 1 fractional rect fill, ANTIALIASED)
                                                        -> 2x pixman_fill, NOTHING ELSE
     text    (4 lines of norns.ttf at 8px, 96 glyphs)   -> 96 composites, one per glyph
                                                           a8 mask at aa=1, a1 mask at aa=0

   The geom scene never reaches a pixman compositor at all. Every antialiased
   edge in it is blended by cairo itself, in _fill_xrgb32_lerp_spans /
   _inplace_spans (cairo-image-compositor.c:2360-2405), which write the
   destination with lerp8x4() without calling pixman. pixman only gets the two
   fully-covered interior boxes, as solid 32-bit fills.

   (19x6 at aa=1 vs 21x7 at aa=0 is the fractional rectangle at
   70.25,50.5,20.5,6.75: antialiased, cairo hands pixman only the fully
   covered interior; unantialiased, it hands over the whole snapped box.)
